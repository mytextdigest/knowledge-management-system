Infra Planning Notes

We could use Redis for the session cache, but it's worth comparing against Memcached before committing to anything. Redis would give us more flexibility with data structures, though it's a bit heavier operationally.

Should we move the session store to a managed service instead of running it ourselves? That's still an open question and we don't have enough information yet to say either way.

We might explore a small pilot with Redis in staging at some point, just to get a feel for it, but nothing has been scheduled and this isn't a commitment either way. If the pilot goes well we could reconsider, but for now this is purely exploratory.
