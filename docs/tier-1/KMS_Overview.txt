================================================================
 KMS: AN AI-POWERED ENTERPRISE KNOWLEDGE PLATFORM
 What it is, how it differs from MTD, and the Tier 1 feature set
================================================================


----------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT IS KMS?
----------------------------------------------------------------

KMS (Knowledge Management System) is an AI-powered Enterprise
Knowledge Management System — the organization's long-term memory.

  - Search & Ask
    Employees search and ask natural-language questions across
    the org's knowledge.

  - Understands Content
    AI reads and reasons about documents — not just storage
    and file names.

  - Discovers Expertise
    Surfaces who knows what, and connects related knowledge
    automatically.

  - Covers Everything
    Policies, SOPs, reports, meeting notes, research — knowledge
    accumulated over years.


----------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW IS KMS DIFFERENT FROM MTD?
----------------------------------------------------------------

MTD (My Text Digest)
  - Per-user document summarization tool
  - Personal, individual workflow
  - Manual summarization only
  - No org structure — no departments, projects, or roles
  - No cross-document intelligence

KMS
  - Org-wide Enterprise Knowledge Management System
  - Multi-tenant: orgs, departments, projects, RBAC roles
  - Automated ingestion (e.g. SharePoint sync) + auto-classification
  - Cross-document reasoning, summarization, org-wide AI chat
  - Relationship/expertise discovery + knowledge health monitoring

Note: Same underlying stack (Next.js, Prisma, AWS) — different
product direction, built as a greenfield platform.


----------------------------------------------------------------
3. TIER 1 FEATURES — IN ORDER OF IMPORTANCE
----------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Conversational Knowledge Assistant
     Org-wide AI chat with streaming, citations & confidence
     scoring.

  2. Semantic Knowledge Retrieval
     Vector-based search across all organizational content.

  3. Automated Knowledge Ingestion Pipeline
     Auto-sync from sources like SharePoint into a unified
     review queue.

  4. Automatic Knowledge Classification
     Auto-categorizes documents, flags duplicates & suggests
     department.

  5. Cross-Document Reasoning
     Auto-organizes related documents into Topics.

  6. Knowledge Summarization
     AI-generated summaries for every document.

  7. Enterprise Knowledge Repository
     Org / department / project structure for all knowledge.

  8. Knowledge Context Engine
     Related-document & expert discovery across the org.
