## Presentation Goal

Create a customer-facing presentation for **KMS**, a product of **Next Generation Innovation L.L.C. (NGI)**.

The presentation should explain:

- What KMS is.
- Why it matters.
- How it works.
- What makes it different.
- Which features have been implemented through Tier 2, including Rank 15 Project Intelligence.

Do **not** present KMS as a simple document repository or chatbot. Present it as an **Enterprise Knowledge Operating System** that turns organizational documents, decisions, lessons, projects, topics, and people into connected intelligence.

## Suggested Deck Length

Target: **14-16 slides**

Audience: prospective customers, executives, department leaders, knowledge managers, IT stakeholders.

Tone: consultative and strategic, not overly technical.

## Core Message

**KMS turns scattered enterprise knowledge into connected organizational intelligence.**

Supporting message:

Most organizations do not lose knowledge because documents are missing. They lose knowledge because context is missing:

- who knows the topic,
- what is related,
- why decisions were made,
- what was learned,
- which project used which knowledge,
- and how topics are changing over time.

KMS solves this by moving the enterprise from:

**Documents -> Knowledge -> Intelligence -> Organizational Memory**
