# KMS Product Requirement Document

## Positioning

An Enterprise Knowledge Operating System is not primarily about storing knowledge. It is about:

1. Capturing organizational knowledge.
2. Understanding organizational knowledge.
3. Connecting organizational knowledge.
4. Delivering organizational knowledge in context.
5. Learning from organizational knowledge.
6. Driving organizational decisions.

Under this model, search becomes important, but not the primary capability. The primary capability becomes **Knowledge Intelligence**.

## What Investors and Buyers Will Care About

If you are pitching this platform, the most compelling hierarchy is:

**Level 1 — Ask**

- Conversational Knowledge Assistant
- Semantic Retrieval

**Level 2 — Understand**

- Topic Modeling
- Auto Classification
- Summarization
- Cross-Document Intelligence

**Level 3 — Connect**

- Expert Discovery
- Knowledge Graph
- Decision Intelligence

**Level 4 — Learn**

- Lessons Learned
- Topic Evolution
- Knowledge Gap Detection

**Level 5 — Act**

- Predictive Knowledge Delivery
- Operational Guidance
- Decision Support

This progression moves the product from:

**Document Management System → Knowledge Management System → Knowledge Intelligence Platform → Enterprise Knowledge Operating System**

which is where the strongest strategic differentiation lies.

## Product Differentiation

From a commercialization perspective, the strongest differentiator in your architecture is actually **automatic topic discovery + Bhattacharyya-distance-based document domain assignment + conversational retrieval**. Many platforms have vector search and chatbots today; far fewer can automatically build and maintain a meaningful enterprise knowledge taxonomy without extensive human curation. That capability should be highlighted as a top-tier differentiator in the product strategy and messaging.
